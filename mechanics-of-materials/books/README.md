## Reading map

| Repository topics | MIT Roylance module titles | Virginia Tech chapters |
|---|---|---|
| Stress, strain and material behavior | Introduction to Elasticity; Stress-Strain Curves | 2–4 |
| Equilibrium, axial structures and composites | Trusses; Introduction to Composites | 1, 5 |
| Circular torsion | Shear and Torsion | 6 |
| Beam forces, section geometry and stresses | Shear and Bending Moment Diagrams; Stresses in Beams | 7–10 |
| Beam deflection and energy concepts | Beam Displacements | 11 |
| Thin pressure vessels | Pressure Vessels | 13 |
| Transformations and elasticity | Kinematics; Equilibrium; Tensor Transformations; Constitutive Relations | 12, 14 |
| Yield and plastic behavior | Yield and Plastic Flow | material/combined-load foundations |
| Fatigue and fracture | Fatigue; Introduction to Fracture Mechanics | seek specialist follow-on reading |
| Creep and time dependence | Dislocation Basis of Yield and Creep; Linear Viscoelasticity | seek specialist follow-on reading |
| Composite plates | Laminated Composite Plates | seek specialist follow-on reading |
| Numerical methods and elasticity | Closed-Form Solutions; Finite Element Analysis | foundational chapters |
| Columns | Supplement with a dedicated stability treatment | 15 |

Not every repository topic has a full matching module in the attached book. Curved beams, spring design, restrained warping, practical fatigue design and code-based stability are specialist extensions; the concise notes identify their model limits.
